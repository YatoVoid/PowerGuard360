# Project story (egetwasm)

The story of how we built Power Guard 360, for the Devpost write-up, the pitch, and the DEV writing challenge after the event.

## Write
- `LOG.md`: a running log with timestamps. Decisions, what broke, what we changed our minds about, who solved what.
- `STORY.md`: the narrative, built from the log near the end. What inspired it, how we built it, challenges, what we learned, what's next. Devpost asks for exactly these sections.
- Screenshots and photos of the team and the hardware go in `story/media/`.

The judges score "Learning", so note every time someone learns something new or gets stuck.
